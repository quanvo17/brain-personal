# Event

A time-based occurrence such as a meeting, workshop, class, review, exam, conference, or other scheduled activity.

**Use when:** something happens at a specific date, time, or identifiable period and may produce notes, decisions, actions, or follow-ups.

**Do not use when:** it's a recurring process itself (use [[Operation]] instead), or it's knowledge extracted from the event (use [[Note]] instead).
