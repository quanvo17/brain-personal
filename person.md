# Person

A person who is relevant to projects, events, knowledge, learning, collaboration, or professional relationships.

**Use when:** a person appears repeatedly in your work or knowledge graph and you may want to track interactions, context, roles, or relationships with them.

**Do not use when:** it's for every person mentioned in a document or meeting. Avoid creating Person records for one-time or insignificant contacts unless future context is likely to be useful.
