# Operation

A recurring process, routine, workflow, or standard way of working used to maintain responsibilities or support projects.

**Use when:** an activity is performed repeatedly using a similar process, checklist, cadence, or workflow.

**Do not use when:** it's a one-time initiative (use [[Project]] instead), a single action (use [[Task]] instead), or a specific occurrence of the routine (use [[Event]] instead).
