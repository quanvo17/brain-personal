# Topic

A long-term subject, domain, concept, technology, or area of knowledge used to organize and connect related knowledge.

**Use when:** a subject is expected to accumulate multiple notes, projects, events, references, or related concepts over time.

**Do not use when:** it's a single piece of information (use [[Note]] instead), or an outcome you want to achieve (use [[Project]] instead). Avoid creating overly specific Topics when only one or two notes exist.
