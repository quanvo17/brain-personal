# Responsibility

A long-term area of responsibility or role that requires ongoing attention and has no clear end date.

**Use when:** something represents an ongoing area you are responsible for maintaining over time, such as work leadership, professional development, health, or language learning.

**Do not use when:** the work has a specific outcome or clear completion point (use [[Project]] instead), or it represents a knowledge domain (use [[Topic]] instead).
